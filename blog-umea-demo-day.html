ATIRA at Umeå Demo Day

We brought our roguelike to MIT-huset at Umeå University and put the demo in players' hands.

From our screens to players' hands

Demo Day gave us a chance to watch people discover ATIRA for themselves. We spent time at the demo table watching runs, hearing reactions and talking through players' feedback.

Those conversations gave us plenty of notes to take back into development. Seeing how people approach the arena is a useful part of building the game, and we're grateful to everyone who took the time to play and share their thoughts.

Thank you for stopping by

Thank you to everyone who visited our table at MIT-huset. We're looking forward to putting what we learned into the next steps for ATIRA.
