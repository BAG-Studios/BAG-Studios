ATIRA: A Sinner's Paradise

An action roguelike by BAG Studios. The Windows demo is an early look at the game in development.

Enter an abandoned city where criminals fight for their freedom in a brutal colosseum. Survive waves of enemies, face fan-favourite contestants, and fight your way towards becoming the arena's next legend.

Play the Windows demo Join our Discord

Fight through the arena

ATIRA combines third-person ranged and melee combat with movement and abilities. Each wave brings different enemies: some close the distance with shields and melee attacks, while others attack from range and keep you moving. Mini-bosses interrupt the action and test how you adapt.

Wave-based combat

Take on increasingly challenging encounters with short breaks between rounds.

Find your rhythm

Combine shooting, melee attacks, movement and abilities to survive.
